# Assessment requirements (from the Canvas page, marking scheme and cover sheet)

## The assignment
- **Unit:** ENS6160.4 (Semester 2) — Signals and Systems; unit outcomes ULOs 1–4.
- **Assignment:** *Laboratory 2: Frequency Response of Systems* — worth **6%** (6 points).
- **Type:** Group submission, no word limit, unlimited attempts, **due Sunday 25 Oct 2026, 23:59**
  (available 27 Jul 2026 → 26 Oct 2026 08:00).
- **Hand-in:** upload one file on Canvas (the page also has Webcam/Canvas Files options) after ticking the
  academic-integrity declaration. Any use of generative AI must be acknowledged in a statement at the end:
  which tool, which version, and how it was used.
- **Mandatory cover sheet:** the Groupwork coversheet. Reports not using it get **zero**.
  A full lab report in **IMRaD** (or similar general scientific) layout must be submitted, not just the result sheet.

## Required report structure
1. Title page · 2. Abstract (200–400 words) · 3. Table of contents · 4. Introduction · 5. Method ·
6. Results · 7. Discussion · 8. Conclusion (~200 words) · 9. References (APA)

## Marking scheme weights
| Criterion | Weight | What earns top marks |
|-----------|-------:|----------------------|
| Abstract | 5% | Motivation, aim, method, results and implications in a concise summary |
| Introduction | 10% | Context + relevant background theory/literature; aim stated at the end |
| Method | 20% | Repeatable detail, equations used, assumptions stated and supported |
| Results | 20% | All data in tables/figures, each captioned and referred to in the text |
| Discussion | 20% | Insightful analysis of **all three options** (here: sine-wave time domain, sine-wave spectrum, square wave), links to theory, limitations discussed |
| Conclusion | 5% | Aim, main findings, limitations, broader implications |
| Written skills | 15% | Technical vocabulary, style, logical flow, error-free writing |
| Referencing | 5% | Accurate APA citations |

Grade bands: HD 80–100 · D 70–79 · C 60–69 · P 50–59 · F 0–49.

## Group-work cover sheet
Declares unit/assessment details and a table of **names, student numbers, relative contribution (%
or "equally") and signatures**. Anyone contributing <10% should not be listed. All members are
**equally accountable** for the integrity of the whole submission (not just their own part).
The sheet warns about signs of copying/collusion and unacknowledged generative-AI use.
